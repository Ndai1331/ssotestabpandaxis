# Phase 03 — Survey collection and result UX

## Requirements

- Port the paid-source public page flow: location/criteria cards, 1–5 stars,
  respondent modal, optional photo, submit, and thank-you state.
- Preserve the free client visual language and accessibility.
- Add location-filtered SurveyResult statistics, distribution, criteria averages,
  and session detail data.

## Success criteria

- /survey-collections/{locationId} is anonymous and usable on desktop/mobile.
- /survey-results shows both statistics and details without exposing public
  collection controls to unauthorized users.
